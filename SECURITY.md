# Security

Agents act with people's rights and read what they bring in, so reports of vulnerabilities are welcome: a run that
acts with more than its rights, writes after reading outside content, or carries data out through what it writes.

**Please don't open a public issue for a vulnerability.** Report it privately instead: on this repository's
**Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** (GitHub's private vulnerability reporting), with what you found,
how to reproduce it, and the version. We will answer within a week, agree on a fix and a date to publish it, and credit
you unless you'd rather not be named.

Supported versions: the latest release only, while AbpAgent is in preview (0.x).
